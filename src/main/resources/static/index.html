      GAME GUIDE

      Learn about the actors, environments, items and systems you will encounter during your mission.

      ACTORS
      • ContractedWorker (ඞ): Player character with health, inventory, maximum carrying capacity of 50 units, and the ability to explore, collect resources, trade, teleport, consume items, use weapons and interact with the environment; can be infected and affected by traps.
      • Undead (Ѫ): Hostile creature with 15 HP that wanders and attacks nearby Workers for 1 damage with a 10% hit chance, hunts Workers when the alarm activates, dies instantly when infected by a Parasite, and can be spawned by holes and trees.
      • Slime (⍾): Non-hostile creature with 25 HP that wanders, consumes consumable items from the ground and forces adjacent Workers to drop their inventory when spawned.
      • Parasite (x): Creature with 30 HP that infects one adjacent valid target before dying; can be instantly killed by the Parasite Rifle.
      • Scrap Snatcher (s): Creature with 25 HP that moves randomly, collects depositable resources, and becomes aggressive when infected by a Parasite.
      • Weapon Dealer: Non-hostile NPC that wanders Moon 99-Deprecated and offers three weapons to adjacent Workers.
      • Security Drone (optional): Aggressive creature that hunts and attacks Workers and other creatures when the alarm activates.
      • NPC: Base class for creatures that can trigger traps, receive damage and status effects, and stop moving or attacking while stunned.
      GROUNDS
      • Wall (#): Blocks Workers from passing through.
      • Floor (_): Allows Workers to walk across the facility and transforms into ScorchedEarth when caught in a Fire Mine explosion.
      • Door (=, N, M): Aluminium, Iron and Titanium doors requiring AccessCard Levels 1, 2 and 3 respectively; unlocking causes different effects, including damage, fire or healing; Aluminium Doors can also be cut using a Plasma Cutter.
      • Puddle (~): Allows Workers standing on it to drink toxic or purified water depending on whether they carry a SterilisationBox.
      • Fire (^): Burns actors standing on it, dealing 1 damage per turn for 5 turns.
      • Hole (O): Spawns creatures every 20 turns; can spawn Undead, Slimes or Scrap Snatchers on 99-Deprecated, and Undead, Parasites or Scrap Snatchers on 20-Overflow; has a 1% chance to expand after successfully spawning.
      • Vent (V): Spawns Parasites, Slimes or Scrap Snatchers when a Worker is nearby, poisons adjacent actors after successfully spawning, and can be cut with a Plasma Cutter to produce an Industrial Fan and spawn an Undead.
      • Teleportation Tube (Φ): Teleports Workers to predetermined locations within or between maps, with a 50% malfunction chance and fire spawning at the destination.
      • Magic Circle (◎): Randomly teleports Workers to another Magic Circle on the same map and spawns a Flask beside the destination.
      • Toxic Waste (≈): Damages any actor standing on it for 1 HP per turn.
      • Fleshy Tree Sprout (y): On 99-Deprecated, has a 25% chance to grow into a Mature Tree every 20 turns and spawns an Undead when a Worker approaches; on other maps, it spawns a Slime and grows into a Sapling.
      • Fleshy Tree Sapling (v): Grows into a Mature Tree with a 50% chance every 25 turns.
      • Fleshy Tree Mature Tree (Y): On 99-Deprecated, spawns a Scrap Snatcher when approached and has a 50% chance to grow into a Fleshy Monolith every 35 turns; on other maps, it spawns an Undead when approached.
      • Fleshy Monolith (H): Cannot grow further and teleports any adjacent Worker to a random valid location on the same map every turn.
      • Warper Tree Sapling (w): Has a 25% chance to grow into a Mature Tree every 20 turns.
      • Warper Tree Mature Tree (W): Automatically teleports nearby Workers to a random location on the map.
      • Supercomputer (≡): Shop terminal that handles Worker Credit purchases and sales, accepts Company Credit deposits, tracks quota deadlines and makes adjacent Workers unconscious if they fail to meet the quota.
      • Alarm Tile (optional): Triggers the facility alarm when a Worker steps onto it.
      • IceTile: Temporarily replaces the target's ground for 5 turns, deals 1 cold damage and applies frostbite (1 damage per turn for 3 turns), then restores the original ground.
      • PoisonGround: Temporarily replaces a killed Parasite's tile for 6 turns and applies PoisonStatus (2 damage per turn for 3 turns) to actors stepping onto it.
      • ScorchedEarth (⌂): Hazardous terrain created by Fire Mine explosions that continuously damages actors standing on it.
      ITEMS
      • Flask (u): Can be drunk five times, restoring 1 HP per use, and remains in the inventory when empty.
      • AccessCard Level 1 (▤): Weighs 1 unit and unlocks Aluminium Doors.
      • AccessCard Level 2 (α): Weighs 2 units, unlocks Aluminium and Iron Doors, and deals 5 damage when purchased.
      • AccessCard Level 3 (◐): Weighs 3 units, unlocks all doors, and has a 50% chance of deducting an additional 50 credits when purchased.
      • FirstAidKit (+): Permanently increases maximum health by 1, fully heals the Worker, has a 20-turn cooldown and costs 1,000 credits.
      • SterilisationBox (▣): Allows Workers to sterilise consumable items and grounds; costs 750 credits and removes one random inventory item when purchased.
      • Apple (ó): Weighs 1 unit; heals 3 HP if sterilised, otherwise poisons the consumer for 5 turns (1 damage per turn); sells for 1 credit.
      • Cookies (◍): Weighs 2 units and has 5 uses; each cookie reduces maximum HP by 1 or heals 1 HP if sterilised; sells for 1 credit per remaining cookie and loses 1 cookie per turn when infected.
      • Lantern (&): Weighs 7 units, has 10 oil units and a 5% chance each turn while carried to leak and ignite the ground beneath the Worker; sells for 5 credits per remaining oil unit and loses 1 oil per turn when infected.
      • Floppy Disk (⊟): Weighs 1 unit and sells for 1 credit, with a 50% chance of deducting 50 additional credits.
      • CRT Monitor (◙): Weighs 30 units and sells for 25 credits; heals the seller by 5 HP, with a 20% chance of dealing 2 damage and spawning Fire on surrounding tiles.
      • Alien Cube (◈): Can be carried and used to teleport to 1 of 3 random locations on the current map, permanently turning adjacent source tiles into Toxic Waste; sells for 25 credits but spawns an Undead beside the seller; can be cut with a Plasma Cutter to produce an Alien Artifact and poison the Worker.
      • Plasma Cutter (>): Weighs 7 units, costs 50 Worker Credits, deals 5 immediate damage and burns the buyer for 5 turns; allows Workers to cut Aluminium Doors, Vents and Alien Cubes.
      • Aluminium Scrap (%): Weighs 2 units; can be deposited for 50 Company Credits, with a 20% chance of dealing 5 damage to the Worker.
      • Industrial Fan (@): Weighs 5 units; can be sold for 150 Worker Credits (spawns a Slime near the Supercomputer) or deposited for 10 Company Credits (heals the Worker by 10 HP).
      • Alien Artifact (?): Weighs 1 unit; can be sold for 200 Worker Credits (50% chance of poisoning the Worker) or deposited for 100 Company Credits (teleports the Worker to a random valid location on the same map).
      • Electric Rod: Wieldable weapon that deals 5 damage to the primary target and triggers chain lightning, dealing 3 damage to every other actor adjacent to the wielder.
      • Frost Blade: Wieldable weapon that deals 6 damage to the primary target and transforms its tile into an IceTile for 5 turns.
      • Parasite Rifle: Wieldable weapon that scans the entire row in the chosen direction, instantly kills the first Parasite found and creates PoisonGround on its tile; misses trigger a 15-turn cooldown.
      • FireMine (☼): Unpickable proximity trap that detonates when an actor enters its tile, dealing 10 instant damage and applying BurnStatus (2 damage per turn for 3 turns) within its 3×3 blast radius.
      • GasMine (☁): Unpickable proximity trap that detonates when an actor enters its tile, dealing 5 instant damage and applying PoisonStatus (1 damage per turn for 5 turns) within its 3×3 blast radius.
      • StunMine (⚡): Unpickable proximity trap that detonates when an actor enters its tile, dealing no physical damage but applying StunStatus for 2 turns within its 3×3 blast radius.
      OTHER COMPONENTS
      • Facility Alarm System: Detects alarm triggers and activates consequences, such as making Undead hunt Workers and temporarily locking doors.
      • Alarm State: Tracks whether the alarm is active and how many turns remain before it stops.
      • Quota System: Starts at Company Rank 1 with a quota of 100 Company Credits and a 200-turn deadline; when completed, increases the quota by 5% and the time limit by 10%, rounding both upwards.
      • Company Credits: Separate currency used exclusively to meet the quota through resource deposits.
      • Worker Credits: Personal currency used by Workers to purchase items from the Supercomputer.
      • Infected Status: Applies different effects depending on the host: damages Workers, kills Undead, drains Lantern oil, reduces Cookie quantity and spawns Parasites, or makes Scrap Snatchers aggressive while damaging them by 1 HP per turn.
      • Creature Spawn Reaction: Every newly spawned creature triggers its corresponding reaction: Undead gain 1 maximum HP per adjacent creature, Slimes force adjacent Workers to drop all carried items, and Parasites deal 2 damage to adjacent Workers.
      • Loot Explosion: When a Scrap Snatcher spawns, places one randomly selected depositable resource on every valid adjacent tile.
      • Poison Status: Deals damage over time when applied by infections, weapon effects or resource sales.
      • Burn Status: Deals damage over time when applied by Plasma Cutter purchases or Fire Mine explosions.
      • Stun Status: Prevents affected actors from performing normal actions for 2 turns.
      • Detonatable Interface: Defines how trap items detect actors on their tile and initiate explosions.
      • TrapTriggerable Interface: Defines how Grounds and Actors respond to trap explosion effects.
      • TrapExplosionAction: Calculates the 3×3 blast radius, applies damage and status effects, transforms affected terrain and removes the detonated trap.
      • Wieldable Interface: Defines the behaviour required for weapons that Workers can carry and use to attack.
      • WeaponEffect (Abstract Class): Defines the common structure for applying special weapon effects after a successful attack.
      • ChainLightningEffect: Applies chain lightning damage to adjacent actors and removes secondary victims killed by the attack.
      • FrostEffect: Replaces the target's ground with IceTile and applies cold damage and frostbite.
      • ParasiteBlastEffect: Removes the targeted Parasite and replaces its location with PoisonGround.
      • WieldAttackAction: Executes weapon attacks against valid targets and triggers their corresponding effects.
      • BuyWeaponAction: Deducts Worker Credits and adds the selected weapon to the Worker's inventory.
      • Weapon Cooldown: Tracks the Parasite Rifle's 15-turn cooldown after a missed shot.
      • PoisonGround Duration: Tracks the 6-turn lifetime of PoisonGround tiles.
      • IceTile Duration: Tracks the 5-turn lifetime of IceTile tiles.
      • Console Messages: Prints descriptive messages for purchases, deposits, sales, healing, teleportation, explosions and status effects.
      • Trap Trigger Detection: Checks whether an actor has stepped onto a trap item during the engine tick cycle.
